# About us

Northwind Tools is a two-person shop in Lancaster, Pennsylvania. We make
chisels, marking gauges and the odd mallet, and we sharpen anything you
bring in on a Saturday.

<aside class="note">
  Our workshop is open to visitors on the first Saturday of every month.
</aside>

## What we believe

- A tool should outlive the person who bought it.
- Repairs are part of the job, not an upsell.
